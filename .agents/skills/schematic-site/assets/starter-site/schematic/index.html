Passive RC low-pass

The series resistor and shunt capacitor form a single-pole low-pass. Activate a component to learn what it does here and how its value affects the cutoff.

Signal path

At low frequency, C1's reactance is high and the output follows the input. As frequency rises, C1 presents a lower impedance to ground and attenuates the output. At the calculated cutoff, the ideal unloaded magnitude is approximately −3 dB.
