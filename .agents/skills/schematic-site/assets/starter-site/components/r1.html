Component detail

R1 · 10 kΩ

Series resistor

Purpose in this circuit

R1 isolates the source from C1 and establishes the filter's resistance. Together, R1 and C1 set the ideal unloaded cutoff. Increasing R1 lowers cutoff but raises output impedance and sensitivity to load resistance.

Value rationale

With C1 = 10 nF, the ideal cutoff is 1 / (2π × 10 kΩ × 10 nF) = 1591.5 Hz. The fitted standard value is exactly the value used in that nominal calculation.

calculated · nominal values

Substitution and failure

A close resistance substitute moves cutoff inversely with resistance. An open R1 removes the signal path. A short R1 leaves the source driving C1 directly and makes source impedance part of the filter.
