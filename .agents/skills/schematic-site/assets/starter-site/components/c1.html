Component detail

C1 · 10 nF

Shunt capacitor

Purpose in this circuit

C1 provides the frequency-dependent path to ground. Its reactance falls with frequency, so the output node is attenuated progressively above the pole formed with R1.

Value rationale

With R1 = 10 kΩ, 10 nF produces the ideal nominal cutoff of approximately 1.59 kHz. Capacitance tolerance and dielectric behavior move the real cutoff; those properties need selection and verification in a real design.

provisional · dielectric unspecified

Substitution and failure

A larger capacitor lowers cutoff. An open C1 removes intentional filtering. A short C1 holds the output node at ground through R1.
